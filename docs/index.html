Why Hami Panel?

Other panels share one blind spot: when they generate a broken link, they don't notice. Hami Panel connects through the very link it made and confirms traffic passes — before the customer ever receives it. Includes a Telegram sales bot with wallets and card-to-card payments, multi-server agents with smart subscription links, quota and device limits, and one-command backup/restore.

  • Canary-tested configs — every link is verified end-to-end before delivery
  • Multi-server from day one — lightweight Go agent per node, unhealthy nodes drop out of sub-links automatically
  • Import from 3x-ui and Marzban — migrate without rebuilding users
  • Xray + sing-box cores — VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC, AnyTLS, Reality, XHTTP
  • Self-contained HP-UI — bilingual dashboard, no CDN, no external assets
